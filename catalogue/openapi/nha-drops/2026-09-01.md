## Ingestion blockers to resolve before merge

1. Original abha-api-v3.yaml and phr-api.yaml do not parse as YAML
   (unquoted {{placeholders}}); the fixed copies parse. Ingest originals
   into .raw/ byte-exact anyway; treat fixed/ as NHA's own correction
   layer with CHANGES.md as the record.
2. fixed/abha-api-v3.yaml has zero operationIds; our indexer fails the
   build on any operation without one. Needs a recorded correction.
3. The duplicate-operationId disambiguation style ('abdm-consent-management
   3 (2)') contains spaces and parentheses; legal YAML, but our stub ids
   derive from operationId and will need normalising.
4. Callback-legs-as-paths must be refolded as webhooks per CONVENTIONS.md,
   or they will double-render as callable endpoints.
