# Who takes part in the UHI network

## In plain words

- **The patient app**, called an End User Application ([EUA](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#eua)). It is
  the app a patient or caregiver uses, such as Aarogya Setu or the
  [ABHA](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#abha) app.
- **The provider platform**, called a Health Service Provider Application
  ([HSPA](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#hspa)). It is the system behind a hospital network, a blood bank,
  a Jan Aushadhi Kendra or an ambulance fleet.
- **The [UHI Gateway](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#uhi-gateway)**, run by the National Health Authority
  ([NHA](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#nha)).
