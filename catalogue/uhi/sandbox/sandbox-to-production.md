# The six onboarding steps from sandbox to production

## In plain words

Onboarding runs in six steps. This page covers the first three. The last three
are on [Go live](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/going-live).

| Step | What you do | What you get | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Express intent to your NHA point of contact | Onboarding kick-off | [Step 1](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/sandbox#1-express-intent) |
| 2 | Generate your key pair with the header generator utility | Public and private key | [Step 2](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/sandbox#2-generate-your-key-pair) |
| 3 | Submit the sandbox registration form with your role, callback URL and public key | Subscriber ID and sandbox access | [Step 3](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/sandbox#3-submit-the-sandbox-registration-form) |
| 4 | Build against the sample payloads and the Gateway spec, then run the service's test cases | Passing sandbox integration | [Go live](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/going-live#1-build-and-run-your-services-test-cases) |
| 5 | Record a demo and request sign-off | Written sign-off | [Go live](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/going-live#2-record-a-demo-and-request-sign-off) |
| 6 | Switch your IDs and callback URL to production values | Live on the production network | [Go live](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/going-live#3-switch-to-production) |
