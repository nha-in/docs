# PMBI, the organisation behind Jan Aushadhi

## In plain words

Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Bureau of India. It runs the [HSPA](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#hspa) for Jan Aushadhi, backed by its Kendra and medicine database. See [Jan Aushadhi](/docs/uhi/v1/services/jan-aushadhi).
