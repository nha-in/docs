# NOTTO, the organisation behind NOTTO Hospital Discovery

## In plain words

National Organ & Tissue Transplant Organisation. It runs the [HSPA](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#hspa) for NOTTO Hospital Discovery, backed by its national registry of authorised hospitals. See [NOTTO Hospital Discovery](/docs/uhi/v1/services/notto).
