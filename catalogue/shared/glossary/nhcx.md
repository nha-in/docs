# NHCX, the National Health Claims Exchange

## In plain words

National Health Claims Exchange, ABDM's network for insurance claims
between providers and payers, with its own sandbox and its own document
set at [hcxsbx.abdm.gov.in](https://hcxsbx.abdm.gov.in). See
[NHCX](/docs/nhcx/v1).

## Before you start

Nothing. A glossary entry assumes no prior reading.

## What happens

Nothing happens here. This entry defines a term, it does not describe a call.

## How you know it worked

You have understood this when you can say which of the three gateways your use case belongs to.

## When it goes wrong

Expecting HIE-CM endpoints to work against NHCX. They are separate
gateways with separate specifications.
