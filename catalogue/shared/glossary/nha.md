# NHA, the National Health Authority

## In plain words

National Health Authority, the government body that runs ABDM, publishes
the specifications these pages are written from, and operates both the
[sandbox](sandbox.md) and the production gateways.

Nothing. A glossary entry assumes no prior reading.
