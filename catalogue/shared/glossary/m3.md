# M3, the consent and fetching milestone

## In plain words

Milestone 3, consent and reading records as an [HIU](hiu.md): raising a
consent request, tracking its status, handling the grant or denial
callback, fetching the [consent artefact](consent-artefact.md),
requesting health information and decrypting what arrives. See
[M3](/docs/hiecm/v3/api/m3).

## Before you start

Nothing. A glossary entry assumes no prior reading.

## What happens

Nothing happens here. This entry defines a term, it does not describe a call.

## How you know it worked

You have understood this when you can say whether your system asks for records it does not hold.

## When it goes wrong

Treating a granted consent as permanent. It carries a date range and a
lifetime, and a patient can revoke it at any point.
