## In plain words

A subscription request is decided once. ABDM-1080 comes back when a
second approve or deny lands on a request that already has an answer,
whether from a retry of your own call or from the patient deciding in
another app first. The message reads
`Subscription request may be already approved or denied`.

## Before you start

Know the subscription request id and which call you sent last for it.

## What happens

Conditions that produce this error: approving after an approval,
denying after an approval, or either after a denial. A timed out approve
that did in fact land, then retried, is the common path.

## How you know it worked

The approve or deny call returns 202 the first time, and the
corresponding callback carries the decision. A repeat is refused with
this code, which is the confirmation that the first one took.

## When it goes wrong

Do not retry. Treat ABDM-1080 on a retry as success of the original
call, and read the subscription's state rather than deciding again. If
you did not send the first decision, the patient did.
