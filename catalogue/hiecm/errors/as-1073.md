## In plain words

A consent artefact carries a validity period. It has passed. Records
cannot be fetched under an expired artefact, and no retry changes that.

## Before you start

Read the artefact's validity before fetching, and treat expiry as an
ordinary end rather than a fault. A person who granted access for a month
meant a month.

## What happens

Conditions that produce this error: a fetch retried long after the
grant, a stored artefact reused past its window, or a queued job that ran
late.

## How you know it worked

A fetch under a currently valid artefact is accepted and the records
arrive.

## When it goes wrong

Raise a fresh consent request. Do not retry against the old artefact,
and do not present the expiry to the person as an error in your
application.
