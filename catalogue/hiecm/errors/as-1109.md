## In plain words

Auto approval is a policy the person can switch off at any time. This
one is already switched off, so the call had nothing to do.

## Before you start

Hold the auto approval id and the policy's current state. An application
that stores the id but not the state has to guess.

## What happens

Conditions that produce this error: a disable pressed twice, a disable
issued from two devices, or a policy disabled elsewhere and a stale
screen still offering the action.

## How you know it worked

Reading the policy shows it disabled, which is the state the call was
asking for.

## When it goes wrong

Treat it as success and refresh the screen. The person asked for the
policy to be off, and it is off.
