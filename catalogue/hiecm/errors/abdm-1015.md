## In plain words

ABDM-1015 is HTTP 400 with the message `Invalid Response`. It comes back
on the calls where you answer the gateway rather than ask it something,
and it means your answer is malformed for that leg. It is not about your
headers or your token.

## Before you start

Have the callback you are answering and the body you sent back.

## What happens

Conditions that produce this error: an acknowledgement body that carries
fields the leg does not allow together, or is missing what it needs. The
recorded case is a consent on-notify sent with both an
`acknowledgement` array and an `error` object. A response leg is one
or the other. The body the gateway accepts for that call is:

```json
{
  "acknowledgement": [{"consentId": "<CONSENT_ARTEFACT_ID>", "status": "OK"}],
  "response": {"requestId": "<REQUEST_ID_OF_THE_NOTIFY_YOU_RECEIVED>"}
}
```

with an entry per artefact in the notification.

## How you know it worked

The on-notify call returns 202 and the consent fetch that follows
delivers the artefact on your bridge.

## When it goes wrong

Send `acknowledgement` on success and `error` on failure, never both.
Echo the notification's `REQUEST-ID` header in `response.requestId`,
and include every artefact id the notification listed.
