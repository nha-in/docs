## In plain words

Onboarding creates a facility record. The registry has found one that
already matches and refused to duplicate it.

## Before you start

Run the deduplicate search before the basic information call. Name,
district and sub district are mandatory on it, which is enough to find
most duplicates.

## What happens

Conditions that produce this error: onboarding attempted without the
search, a retry after a create whose response was lost, or a facility
already onboarded by another product.

## How you know it worked

The deduplicate search returns the existing facility and its id, which
is the id to carry into bridge linkage.

## When it goes wrong

Do not create a second record. Take the existing facility ID and
continue from bridge linkage. Where the existing record is wrong, update
it rather than replacing it.
