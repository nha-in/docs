## In plain words

Discovery asked a facility for records held against this person and the
facility answered with none. That is an answer, not a fault.

## Before you start

Have the person's details as the facility would hold them: the name,
year or date of birth and gender they gave at the desk, which may not be
what they later put in their profile.

## What happens

Conditions that produce this error: a person who never visited that
facility, a visit made under a different name, or details that no longer
match the ones given at registration.

## How you know it worked

Discovery against a facility the person did visit returns care contexts
rather than none.

## When it goes wrong

Show the specified wording, "No health records found", rather than an
error. Offer the person a way to check the details they are searching
with, since a mismatch and a genuine absence look identical from here.
