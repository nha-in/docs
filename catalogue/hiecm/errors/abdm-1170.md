# ABDM-1170, the ABHA address is not valid

## In plain words

ABDM does not accept the [ABHA address](/docs/hiecm/v3/getting-started/glossary#abha-address) you sent. The message is "Invalid ABHA address". An ABHA address matches `^[a-zA-Z0-9][a-zA-Z0-9_.]+[a-zA-Z0-9]@(abdm|sbx)$`. It starts and ends with a letter or digit, may carry dots and underscores in between, and ends with `@abdm` or `@sbx`.

## When it goes wrong

Check the address against the pattern, suffix included. If it matches, confirm the account exists in the environment you are calling before you link against it.
