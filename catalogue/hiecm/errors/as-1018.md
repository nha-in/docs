## In plain words

Registration and login run as a transaction. Each step carries the id
of the one before. That transaction has closed, which is why a step that
worked minutes ago fails now.

## Before you start

Know how long a person has been on the screen. A transaction does not
outlive the flow it was opened for, and a person who left the app and
came back is starting again whether the screen says so or not.

## What happens

Conditions that produce this error: a screen left open, an app resumed
after a long pause, or a retry of one step long after the previous step
succeeded.

## How you know it worked

The flow restarted from its first step is accepted, and the later steps
carry the new transaction id.

## When it goes wrong

Start the flow again rather than retrying with the same id. Where this
happens often, the screen is holding people too long between steps.
