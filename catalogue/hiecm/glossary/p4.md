# P4 Locker: keep a person's records for the long term

## In plain words

ABDM Milestone P4, the fourth of the four [PHR](/docs/hiecm/v3/getting-started/glossary#phr)
milestones, implemented through the HIE-CM gateway: a health locker is a PHR
app that keeps a person's records for the long term, under their control. It
hears about new records through a subscription on their
[ABHA address](abha-address.md), fetches them with consent, and stores them.
It builds on [P3](p3.md), adding the locker setup and settings calls, and
applies to an app that stores records rather than only displaying them. See
[P4 Locker](/docs/hiecm/v3/milestones/p4).

## Before you start

Nothing. A glossary entry assumes no prior reading.

## What happens

Nothing happens here. This entry defines a term, it does not describe a call.

## How you know it worked

You have understood this when you can say whether your PHR app stores records or only shows them, and so whether it needs P4.

## When it goes wrong

Leaving P4 out of the count. A PHR app has four milestones, and an app that
keeps records needs the fourth. Processing documents the person uploads
needs certification as a Health Locker, a [Health Repository Provider](hrp.md),
which requires [M2](m2.md).
