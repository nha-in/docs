# M4 Registry Integration: Register Healthcare Professionals and Health Facilities

## In plain words

ABDM Milestone 4, implemented through the HIE-CM gateway, the
registries: creating an [HPID](hpid.md) on the
[HPR](hpr.md) and onboarding a facility to the [HFR](hfr.md). Also
called NHPR, the National Healthcare Professionals and Facilities
Registry. See [M4](/docs/hiecm/v3/api/m4).

## Before you start

Nothing. A glossary entry assumes no prior reading.

## What happens

Nothing happens here. This entry defines a term, it does not describe a call.

## How you know it worked

You have understood this when you can say whether your system registers professionals or facilities.

## When it goes wrong

Leaving M4 until last. Facility onboarding is reviewed by NHA and can
take days, so a late start blocks go live.
