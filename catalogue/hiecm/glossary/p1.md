# P1 Registration and login: the patient side of M1

## In plain words

ABDM Milestone P1, the first of the four [PHR](/docs/hiecm/v3/getting-started/glossary#phr)
milestones, implemented through the HIE-CM gateway: how a person registers
in your PHR app by creating an [ABHA address](abha-address.md), with or
without an ABHA number, and how they log in, through the login routes NHA
makes mandatory. It mirrors [M1](m1.md) from the patient's side. See
[P1 Registration and login](/docs/hiecm/v3/milestones/p1).

## Before you start

Nothing. A glossary entry assumes no prior reading.

## What happens

Nothing happens here. This entry defines a term, it does not describe a call.

## How you know it worked

You have understood this when you can say which milestone a PHR app needs before it can do anything else.

## When it goes wrong

Building the login for one route only. Every documented login route is
required, and a person may arrive by any of them.
