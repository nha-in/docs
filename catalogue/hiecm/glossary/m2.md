# M2 Health Information Provider: Create and link records

## In plain words

ABDM Milestone 2, implemented through the HIE-CM gateway, sharing
records as a [HIP](hip.md): turning your records
into [FHIR](fhir.md) bundles, grouping them into
[care contexts](/docs/hiecm/v3/concepts/care-context), linking those to a patient's
[ABHA address](abha-address.md), answering [discovery](discovery.md), and
encrypting and pushing data when consent allows. See
[M2](/docs/hiecm/v3/api/m2).

## Before you start

Nothing. A glossary entry assumes no prior reading.

## What happens

Nothing happens here. This entry defines a term, it does not describe a call.

## How you know it worked

You have understood this when you can say whether your system holds records that other systems should be able to find.

## When it goes wrong

Assuming M2 alone makes records visible. Nothing you hold is
discoverable until a care context is linked.
