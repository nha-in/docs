# P2 Consents Management: the patient side of M2

## In plain words

ABDM Milestone P2, the second of the four [PHR](/docs/hiecm/v3/getting-started/glossary#phr)
milestones, implemented through the HIE-CM gateway: how a PHR app finds the
records a facility holds about the person, links those
[care contexts](/docs/hiecm/v3/concepts/care-context) to their
[ABHA address](abha-address.md) with the OTP the facility sends, and shares
the person's profile at a facility counter. It mirrors [M2](m2.md) from the
patient's side. See [P2 Consents Management](/docs/hiecm/v3/milestones/p2).

## Before you start

Nothing. A glossary entry assumes no prior reading.

## What happens

Nothing happens here. This entry defines a term, it does not describe a call.

## How you know it worked

You have understood this when you can say that linking a record in P2 does not fetch it: reading it is P3.

## When it goes wrong

Expecting the records to arrive once they are linked. Linking makes a care
context findable against the address; fetching it needs the consent and
data flow of [P3](p3.md).
