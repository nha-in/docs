# P3 Subscription: the patient side of M3

## In plain words

ABDM Milestone P3, the third of the four [PHR](/docs/hiecm/v3/getting-started/glossary#phr)
milestones, implemented through the HIE-CM gateway: a PHR app subscribes to
changes on the person's [ABHA address](abha-address.md), lets them set an
auto approval policy, grant, deny and revoke consent, and fetches, decrypts
and shows the records a granted [consent artefact](consent-artefact.md)
covers. It mirrors [M3](m3.md) from the patient's side. See
[P3 Subscription](/docs/hiecm/v3/milestones/p3).

## Before you start

Nothing. A glossary entry assumes no prior reading.

## What happens

Nothing happens here. This entry defines a term, it does not describe a call.

## How you know it worked

You have understood this when you can say what a subscription tells the app, and what it does not: that a record exists, never the record itself.

## When it goes wrong

Treating a subscription notification as the record. It says a care context
was linked or updated; the record arrives only through a health information
request under a granted consent.
