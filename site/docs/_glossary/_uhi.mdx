These terms belong to UHI: the applications on either side of a booking, the Gateway between them, and the fields every call carries.

<div className="glossary">

### Audit copy

An exact copy of `on_confirm`, `on_status`, `on_update` or `on_cancel` that a Physical Consultation [HSPA](#hspa) sends to the [UHI Gateway](#uhi-gateway), at `/api/v1/uhi/on_<callback>_audit`. The Gateway does not see [direct calls](#direct-call-p2p), so it receives these copies instead. See [Routes](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/routes#audit-copies).

### <LegacyAnchor id="consumer-uri" /><LegacyAnchor id="provider-uri" />consumer_uri, provider_uri

The callback base URLs in a call's `context` block. `consumer_uri` is the [EUA](#eua)'s, and must share a domain name with `consumer_id`. `provider_uri` is the [HSPA](#hspa)'s. Results never come back on the request; they arrive later on one of these public HTTPS URLs. See [Messages and callbacks](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/messages).

### <LegacyAnchor id="context-domain" />context.domain

The field in every call's `context` block that names the service, such as `nic2004:85111` for Physical Consultation. The [UHI Gateway](#uhi-gateway) sends a search to every [HSPA](#hspa) registered for that domain. See [Services](/docs/uhi/v1/services) for each service's value.

### <LegacyAnchor id="p2p" />Direct call (P2P)

A call the [EUA](#eua) and the [HSPA](#hspa) make to each other, peer to peer, without the [UHI Gateway](#uhi-gateway). Every call from `init` onwards goes this way, to `provider_uri` or `consumer_uri`, signed with the sender's own `Authorization` header. See [Routes](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/routes#direct-between-eua-and-hspa).

### EUA

End User Application: in [UHI](#uhi), the consumer facing side, the app a patient or a caregiver uses to search for a service and book it. It sends a signed request to the UHI gateway and receives responses at its own callback URL. See [UHI](/docs/uhi/v1).

### <LegacyAnchor id="fulfillment-type" />fulfillment.type

The field in `message.intent.fulfillment` that names the kind of service a search asks for, such as `PMJAYHEM` for PM-JAY HEM, `Physical` for Physical Consultation or `EMERGENCY` for Ambulance Booking. See the page for your [service](/docs/uhi/v1/services).

### HSPA

Health Service Provider Application: in [UHI](#uhi), the provider side system that receives requests and responds to them, such as an ambulance operator's dispatch platform, a blood bank management system or a pharmacy's stock system. It is the counterpart of the [EUA](#eua).

### NOTTO

National Organ & Tissue Transplant Organisation. It runs the [HSPA](#hspa) for NOTTO Hospital Discovery, backed by its national registry of authorised hospitals. See [NOTTO Hospital Discovery](/docs/uhi/v1/services/notto).

### PM-JAY HEM

PM-JAY Hospital Empanelment Management: the system that knows which hospitals are empanelled under PM-JAY today. The PM-JAY HEM [HSPA](#hspa) searches it live. See [PM-JAY HEM Hospital Discovery](/docs/uhi/v1/services/pmjay-hem).

### PMBI

Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Bureau of India. It runs the [HSPA](#hspa) for Jan Aushadhi, backed by its Kendra and medicine database. See [Jan Aushadhi](/docs/uhi/v1/services/jan-aushadhi).

### Network registry

The register of UHI participants and their public keys. `POST /api/v1/networkregistry/lookup` returns another participant's public key and details, so you can check its signature. See [Network registry lookup](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/registry-lookup).

### UHI Gateway

The routing layer for UHI discovery, run by [NHA](#nha). It authenticates each search, broadcasts it to every [HSPA](#hspa) registered for the [`context.domain`](#context-domain), and relays each `on_search` to the [EUA](#eua). It never sees the booking calls. See [Routes](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/routes).

</div>
