## List the child ABHA accounts linked to this account {/* #m1-enrolment-list-children */}

Returns the child [ABHA](/docs/hiecm/v3/getting-started/glossary#abha) accounts held under a parent's account. For each child it gives the name, date of birth, gender, ABHA number and ABHA address. Send the parent's `X-token` and your `BENEFIT_NAME`.

<AgentOnly>
**Before you start.** The `X-token` from the child ABHA creation response or from the parent's login, sent as `Bearer <token>`.

**When it goes wrong.** A 401 with `ABDM-1021` means your integration lacks the privilege for this call.
</AgentOnly>
