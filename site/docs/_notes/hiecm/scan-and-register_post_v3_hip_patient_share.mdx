## Profile share callback {/* #p2-profile-share-callback */}

When a person scans your facility's code and agrees to share, ABDM posts their profile here. `metaData.context` names the counter the code belongs to. `profile.patient` holds the details to register the person with.

<AgentOnly>
**Before you start.** A callback URL registered for your bridge and reachable from the public internet.

**What happens.** Answer with `200`, then send the on-share call with a token number for the person.

**How you know it worked.** The person's PHR app shows the token number you sent.
</AgentOnly>

## Receive a patient's shared profile {/* #m1-receive-patient-share */}

Your [Health Information Provider (HIP)](/docs/hiecm/v3/getting-started/glossary#hip) hosts this call, and [ABDM](/docs/hiecm/v3/getting-started/glossary#abdm) makes it. When a person scans your facility's code and agrees to share, ABDM posts their profile to `/api/v3/hip/patient/share`, relative to your bridge callback URL. `X-HIP-ID` names your facility. Answer with 200, then send the on-share acknowledgement.

<AgentOnly>
**What happens.** `intent` is `PROFILE_SHARE`. The date of birth arrives as `dayOfBirth`, `monthOfBirth` and `yearOfBirth`, and the mobile number as `phoneNumber`.

**When it goes wrong.** A slow answer loses the share: ABDM reports `ABDM-1007` when the connection times out. Answer first, then register the person.
</AgentOnly>
