## Edit a subscription {/* #p3-edit-subscription */}

Lets the person change an existing subscription, for example its date range. Send the HIU's id in `hiuId` and the whole new scope in `subscriptionEditAndApprovalRequest`.

<AgentOnly>
**Before you start.** The person's `X-AUTH-TOKEN` and the `subscriptionId` from the approve call or the subscription details. Every source carries a `status`.

**How you know it worked.** You receive `202` with `subscriptionId`.

**When it goes wrong.** `400` with `ABDM-1066` means the token is invalid: log the person in again.
</AgentOnly>
