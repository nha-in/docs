## Get a health locker by its id {/* #p3-patient-locker-details-by-locker-id */}

Returns one health locker's settings for the logged-in person, by its `locker-id`: whether it is `active`, and the `subscriptions` it holds.

<AgentOnly>
**Before you start.** The person's `X-AUTH-TOKEN` and a `lockerId` from the list of subscribed lockers.

**How you know it worked.** You receive `200` with `lockerId`, `lockerName`, `active` and `subscriptions`.

**When it goes wrong.** `400` with `ABDM-1066` means the token is invalid: log the person in again.
</AgentOnly>
