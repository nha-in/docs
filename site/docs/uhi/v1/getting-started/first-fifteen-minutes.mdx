# Quickstart

You find [PM-JAY](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#pm-jay-hem) empanelled
hospitals near a point here, in four steps: enter your IDs, sign, send, and see
the hospitals arrive on your callback.

## Before you start

- **Your sandbox registration.** Your subscriber ID and public key ID, from
  [sandbox registration](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/sandbox).
- **The Header Generation Utility**, with your private key, on your own machine.
- **A callback.** Expose `POST <your consumer_uri>/on_search` on a public HTTPS
  URL. It replies 200 with this body at once, then stores the request:

  ```json
  { "message": { "ack": { "status": "ACK" } }, "error": {} }
  ```

## How this fits your product

Every UHI service your app adds later follows this exchange.

- **Signing, step 2.** Every outbound call carries a fresh signature. See
  [Signing](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/signing).
- **The `ACK`, step 3.** The response to your call is only a receipt. See
  [Messages and callbacks](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/messages).
- **The callback, step 4.** Results arrive on your
  [`consumer_uri`](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#consumer-uri), matched
  by `transaction_id`. Some services answer from many
  [HSPAs](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#hspa), so you aggregate.
- **Other services** change the domain and the filters. Physical Consultation
  and Ambulance Booking add
  [direct calls](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#direct-call-p2p) after
  `on_search`. See [Routes](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/routes).

## If a call fails

Step 4 carries the symptom table. A `200` whose `error` is not empty carries
the reason: [Errors on UHI](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/errors) sets out the object
and what to log.
