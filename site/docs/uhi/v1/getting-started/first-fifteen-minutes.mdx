# Quickstart

You find PM-JAY empanelled hospitals near a point here, in one signed search to
the sandbox [UHI Gateway](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#uhi-gateway).
The exchange touches every part of the
[UHI](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#uhi) network with the smallest
payload: signing, the Gateway, an
[HSPA](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#hspa) and an asynchronous callback.

This page builds the exact request with you. You sign and send it from your own
machine, because the signature needs your private key and the answer arrives on
your own callback URL.

## Before you start

Have these from [sandbox registration](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/sandbox):

- your subscriber ID and public key ID;
- your private key, in the Header Generation Utility on your own machine;
- a public HTTPS URL for callbacks, your
  [`consumer_uri`](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#consumer-uri).

A [PM-JAY HEM](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#pm-jay-hem) GPS search
needs no state.

## How this fits your product

Every UHI service your app adds later follows this exchange. Your app sends a
signed search, shows a waiting state, and renders results as your callback
receives them.

- **Signing, step 3.** Every outbound call carries a fresh signature. See
  [Signing](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/signing).
- **The `ACK`, step 4.** The response to your call is only a receipt. See
  [Messages and callbacks](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/messages).
- **The callback, step 5.** Results arrive on your `consumer_uri`, matched by
  `transaction_id`. Some services answer from many HSPAs, so you aggregate.
- **Discovery services** change the domain and the filters. Physical
  Consultation and Ambulance Booking add
  [direct calls](/docs/uhi/v1/getting-started/glossary#direct-call-p2p)
  between your app and the HSPA after `on_search`. See
  [Routes](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/routes).

[Services](/docs/uhi/v1/services) sets out each service and which to build first.

## If a call fails

Step 5 carries the symptom table for this search. A `401` or `403` on step 4
comes before any body, so start with [Signing](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/signing)
and your registration. A `200` whose `error` is not empty carries the reason:
[Errors on UHI](/docs/uhi/v1/concepts/errors) sets out the object and what to
log.
