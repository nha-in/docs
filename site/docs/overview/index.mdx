# Overview

The Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission ([ABDM](/docs/overview/glossary#abdm)) is India's national
health data network, run by the National Health Authority ([NHA](/docs/overview/glossary#nha)).
It gives a person a portable digital health identity, and it moves health records between the
systems that hold them, with the patient's consent for each transfer.

This portal documents that network for the engineer doing the integration. Start by choosing
your gateway. Everything after that follows from the choice.

:::note[Documented, not verified]
These pages follow NHA's published documents for the simplified milestone flows, the UHI
onboarding services and the PHR application. Nothing here has been run against the ABDM
sandbox from this repository, so treat request and response shapes as unconfirmed.
:::

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your gateway">
    ABDM is not one API. It is three gateways, and each serves a different kind of product.
    The three are compared below. If your product touches a patient record, your gateway is
    [HIE-CM](/docs/overview/building-blocks/hie-cm).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your role">
    A system that creates records is an [HIP](/docs/overview/glossary#hip). A system that
    reads someone else's records is an [HIU](/docs/overview/glossary#hiu). A patient facing
    app is a [PHR](/docs/overview/glossary#phr). One system often takes several roles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get sandbox credentials">
    Register your application on the ABDM sandbox to receive a client id and client secret.
    NHA issues these, and it can take days. [Get started](/docs/overview/get-started) covers
    the registration and what to do while you wait.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first call">
    Exchange the client id and client secret for an access token at the gateway session
    endpoint, then call the module you need. The
    [gateway session reference](/reference/hiecm-gateway) has the request shape.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Requests between an HIP and an HIU do not go direct. They route through the HIE-CM, which is
data blind by design. NHA's Milestone 2 document states that it does not access or store
health record content, and works only with identifiers and metadata about care contexts.

## The three registries

| Registry | What it identifies |
| --- | --- |
| [ABHA](/docs/overview/glossary#abha) | A person. A 14 digit ABHA number is issued after Aadhaar based [KYC](/docs/overview/glossary#kyc). An ABHA address, such as `name@abdm`, is the name used to hold and share records. |
| [HPR](/docs/overview/glossary#hpr) | A healthcare professional, such as a doctor. |
| [HFR](/docs/overview/glossary#hfr) | A health facility, such as a hospital, clinic or lab. |

ABDM does not build a central store of health records. Records stay in the system that
created them. The registries add identity, an address book and a consent layer on top, which
is what makes a record findable, and transferable when the patient agrees.

More detail: [Registries](/docs/overview/building-blocks/registries).

## The four milestones

NHA groups integration work into milestones. You certify against them one at a time.

| Milestone | What you build | Scope here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [M1](/docs/api/hie-cm/m1) | ABHA creation, ABHA login, profile management, sessions | Phase 1 |
| [M2](/docs/api/hie-cm/m2) | Care contexts, linking, discovery, sharing records as an HIP | Phase 1 |
| [M3](/docs/api/hie-cm/m3) | Consent requests, consent artefacts, fetching records as an HIU | Phase 1 |
| [M4](/docs/api/hie-cm/m4) | Professional and facility registries | Phase 2 |
