# Introduction

The Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission ([ABDM](/docs/abdm/v3/glossary#abdm)) is India's national
health data network, run by the National Health Authority ([NHA](/docs/abdm/v3/glossary#nha)).
This section documents the Health Information Exchange and Consent Manager
([HIE-CM](/docs/abdm/v3/glossary#hie-cm)), the gateway that carries identity, care contexts,
consent and health records.

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose your role">
    An [HIP](/docs/abdm/v3/glossary#hip) creates records. An
    [HIU](/docs/abdm/v3/glossary#hiu) reads records held elsewhere. A
    [PHR](/docs/abdm/v3/glossary#phr) is the patient's own app, and NHA's PHR document states
    that every PHR application also implements HIU functionality. One system often takes
    several roles. See [HIP and HIU](/docs/abdm/v3/concepts/hip-hiu) and
    [PHR applications](/docs/abdm/v3/phr).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get sandbox credentials">
    NHA reviews your registration before it issues a client id and client secret, and its
    documents do not say how long that takes. [Sandbox access](/docs/abdm/v3/sandbox) covers
    the registration and the wait.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your milestones">
    NHA certifies four milestones one at a time.
    [Integration milestones](/docs/abdm/v3/milestones) maps each role to the milestones it
    has to build.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your first call">
    Exchange the client id and client secret for an access token at the gateway session
    endpoint. The [gateway session reference](/reference/hiecm-gateway) has the request shape.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The registries

Every HIE-CM call carries an identifier that a registry issued.

| Registry | What it identifies |
| --- | --- |
| [ABHA](/docs/abdm/v3/building-blocks/registries/abha) | A person. A 14 digit [ABHA](/docs/abdm/v3/glossary#abha) number is issued after Aadhaar based [KYC](/docs/abdm/v3/glossary#kyc). An ABHA address, such as `name@abdm`, is the name used to hold and share records. |
| [NHPR](/docs/abdm/v3/building-blocks/registries/nhpr) | The provider side: healthcare professionals in the [HPR](/docs/abdm/v3/glossary#hpr), health facilities in the [HFR](/docs/abdm/v3/glossary#hfr). |

ABDM keeps no central store of health records. Records stay in the system that created them.
HIE-CM is data blind: NHA's Milestone 2 document states that it does not access or store
health record content, and works only with identifiers and metadata about care contexts.
[Registries](/docs/abdm/v3/building-blocks/registries) has the detail.

## The four milestones

| Milestone | What you build |
| --- | --- |
| [M1](/docs/abdm/v3/api/m1) | ABHA creation, ABHA login, profile management, sessions |
| [M2](/docs/abdm/v3/api/m2) | Care contexts, linking, discovery, sharing records as an HIP |
| [M3](/docs/abdm/v3/api/m3) | Consent requests, consent artefacts, fetching records as an HIU |
| [M4](/docs/abdm/v3/api/m4) | Professional and facility registries |
