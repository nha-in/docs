### M2: a correction

The page said the HIP initiated linking request, POST /api/hiecm/hip/v3/link/carecontext, names each patient entry's health information type hiTypes. The field is hiType, one value such as OPConsultation. A body with hiTypes gets no answer from the gateway, not a 400. [Read the page](/docs/pr-114/docs/hiecm/v3/milestones/m2).
